This is the real CamusDB engine — parser, planner, executor and storage — running in your browser tab. Nothing is sent to a server. A sample authors and books data set is loaded; a reload starts over.

Three CamusDB nodes run in this one tab. They replicate through Raft over an in-memory transport. Send a statement to any node, stop a node, or cut its links, and watch the cluster carry on.
